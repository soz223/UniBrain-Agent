TaskForce style

The learned policy observes four task losses and their gradient Gram matrix on bounded parameter probes during labeled training. It uses one actor per task, shared reward, centralized critics and replay. At inference, the trained UniBrain model predicts without losses or policy updates.

When does this change training?

For the full original architecture, enable Learned gradient policy before Confirm & run. Every labeled training update computes four task gradients, predicts their mixture, and trains the policy from the next observation. The original path remains available with the switch off.
