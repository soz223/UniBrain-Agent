When does this change training?

These review buttons produce advice. To coordinate a custom run, enable “Use training roles” in the pipeline controls. Before optimizer update 2, the four specialists review measured task gradients and the coordinator selects whether to retain or rebalance loss weights. Each weight change is bounded and the total budget is preserved. Original UniBrain reproduction keeps its original losses and optimizer.

Roles are implemented as separate prompts and model calls. A learned multi-agent reinforcement learning policy and gradient projection are not implemented.
